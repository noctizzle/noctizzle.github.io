Detective: Truth & Lies

Welcome to the Village of Alibi, where everybody fibs about something. You're the new Sergeant at the front desk of the village police station, and one by one the villagers lean over the counter with a story.

App Store iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo Google Play Android phones and tablets

How it plays

  • Swipe right to believe, left to doubt. Every visitor brings a piece of evidence: a snapshot, a receipt, a bus timetable. Read the face, check the paperwork, make the call.
  • Search the crime scene. When the station phone rings, step into a painted room. A numbered tent marks everything that matters, so there are no pixels to hunt.
  • Prove it on the case board. Fill in the Case Questions with what you heard and found, then charge your suspect and see your verdict on the front page of the village Gazette. Three Big Cases to crack.
  • Learn their tells. An ear that goes red, a rattling teacup: every villager has a habit that gives them away.
  • A new Daily Shift every day, the same for everyone. One missed day a week is forgiven.
  • No energy, no lives, no waiting. Nothing is timed. Most crimes are village-sized; one Big Case turns darker, opens with a content note, and never shows anything graphic.

Want a taste first? Try today's Second opinion: one visitor, one claim, your call.

Help

Something isn't working

Email noctizzle@gmail.com with your phone or tablet model and what happened, or use Settings → Send feedback in the game. We read every message.

An ad looked wrong

On iPhone and iPad, Settings → Report an ad sends us the details. You can also email us. Your ad choices (personalised or not) are under Settings → Privacy Choices where the law offers them.

Your progress and your data

There is no account. Your progress is saved on your device only, and deleting the game deletes it. The privacy policy explains what the game's ads partner, Google AdMob, collects.

The darker case

One Big Case, The Millpond, is darker than the rest. It opens with a content note, nothing graphic is ever shown, and you never need it for a rank: the Daily Shift and Overtime stay as light as ever.

Reminders

The daily reminder is optional and off until you choose a time. Change it or turn it off in Settings → Daily reminder, or in your device's notification settings.
