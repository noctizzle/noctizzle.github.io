Contact us

Email noctizzle@gmail.com. We read every message. Please tell us the game's version and whether you play on an iPhone, an iPad or Android. In the game, Settings → Send feedback writes the email for you, with your game version. Settings is the gear on the home screen.

How to play

Every board is a patchwork of fields. Put one animal in every row, every column and every field, and keep them apart: no two animals may touch, not even at the corners. Each puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by thinking, never by guessing.

  • Tap a square to mark it with a tuft (no animal here), tap again to place an animal, and a third time to clear it.
  • Press and hold a square to place an animal straight away, or to lift one off.
  • Drag across empty squares to mark a whole line of tufts at once. Start the drag on a tuft to rub tufts out instead.
  • Undo as often as you like. There are no timers, no lives and no way to fail.
  • Auto-mark shows lighter tufts on the squares an animal rules out. Switch it off in Settings → Auto-mark for a plainer board.

If two animals clash, they glow red, and so does the row, column or field they share. Every field has its own pattern as well as its own colour, so you never have to tell fields apart by colour alone.

Farms, rosettes and the flock

The Journey is a lane of 40 farms through spring, summer, autumn and winter, with 30 puzzles on each: 1,200 in all, from gentle 5×5 boards to fiendish 10×10 ones. Puzzles open as you solve, so there is always something to try, and a farm opens when you have solved most of the one before. You earn up to three rosettes for a puzzle, and one fewer for using a hint or for a clash that glowed (never fewer than one). Master a whole farm for its ribbon.

Finish a farm and a new animal joins the flock. Choose which one you place from the flock screen. Later farms add ponds, where no animal can stand.

Hints

A hint spotlights the squares that matter and explains the idea in one short sentence. You get three free hints a day (the count starts again at midnight on your device). After those, your spare hints are used, and you can watch a short optional video for one more. Solving the daily puzzle banks a spare hint, and a video doubles it. Finishing a farm offers a video for two more spare hints. You can hold up to nine spare hints. A puzzle you used a hint on earns one rosette fewer.

The daily puzzle

A new puzzle arrives every day, the same for every player. It opens once you have solved the first few Journey puzzles. Solve it on days in a row to build your streak; one missed day a week is forgiven, so the streak carries on. You can share your result as a short note that gives nothing away.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

The daily reminder is optional and stays off until you say yes. The game offers it after your first daily puzzle, or you can turn it on any time in Settings → Daily reminder. You get at most one a day, and none once today's puzzle is done. To stop it, switch off Settings → Daily reminder in the game. You can also turn off notifications for Woolly Logic in your device's Settings → Notifications → Woolly Logic (on Android, Settings → Apps → Woolly Logic → Notifications). Reminders are scheduled on your device, and nothing about them is sent to us.

Report an ad

On iPhone and iPad, use Settings → Report an ad. On Android, email us which ad you saw and when.

Ads and privacy choices

Woolly Logic is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner below the game and an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, never in your first few and never after the daily puzzle. Videos are always optional. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears once you have finished the guided first puzzle, before any ads load, and Settings → Privacy Choices changes your ad consent later. On iPhone and iPad, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking in your device's Settings controls tracking; the game plays the same whichever you choose. More: Privacy Policy.

Sound and music

Sound and Music in the game's Settings turn its sound effects and music on and off. On iPhone the game follows the Ring/Silent switch (Silent mode, on iPhones with an Action button): turn silent off to hear the game. iPads have no Ring/Silent switch, so use Control Centre or the volume buttons. Music you play in another app keeps playing alongside it.

Your progress

There is no account and nothing to sign in to. Your Journey progress, rosettes, the flock, daily results, streak, hints and settings are saved on your device, and deleting the game deletes them. We can't restore them for you, because we never receive them. To see the first puzzle's guide again, use Settings → Replay the first puzzle.

Devices

Woolly Logic plays on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, and works offline. It has no purchases on any of them: everything in the game is earned by playing.
