About the game

Brick Rivals is a neon brick breaker built around head-to-head duels. Two players get the same bricks and 90 seconds: big combos and bomb chains send junk rows crashing onto the rival's board, and the last 15 seconds score double. Play ranked online against other players or race their recorded ghosts, practise against a bot, beat Rival Road's 11 rivals, set a best on the Daily Wall, or see how far an endless Arcade run goes across 11 arenas, each with its own music and signature event. It's free, its ads never interrupt play, every unlock is cosmetic, and it's made by Noctizzle, a solo developer in the UK.

Key features

  • 90-second 1v1 duels. Both players get the same bricks. Big combos, bomb chains and wave clears send junk rows onto the rival's board, a quick reply can BLOCK them, and the FINAL 15 seconds score double.
  • Online ranked, if you want it. Trophies and leagues from Bronze to Champion, a REMATCH after a ranked duel, and ghost races against real players' recorded duels when no rival is online. Online play is optional and uses an anonymous profile with a generated name: no sign-up and no chat.
  • 11 arenas, each with its own twist. Every arena has its own music and a signature event: eruptions that turn bricks into bombs, gravity wells, warp portals, lightning, a board that flips upside down, bounties at HIGH NOON and a CURVEBALL you bend with a swipe. Most arenas play on their own court shape, seven in all.
  • Endless Arcade. Three lives, a new arena every wave, power-ups (multi-ball, wide paddle, fireball, slow-mo and extra lives) and a GUARDIAN of armoured bricks every fifth wave.
  • Daily Wall and Rival Road. One board a day, the same for everyone, with three tries to set a best and a code to challenge a friend; and 11 practice rivals to beat in turn, one for each arena.
  • Fair by design. 26 paddle plates and 15 ball trails to buy with coins or earn by playing, all cosmetic, so nothing anyone unlocks makes them stronger. Ads never show during play, and videos are always optional.
