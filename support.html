Brick Rivals support

Brick Rivals by Noctizzle · for iPhone and Android

Contact us

Email noctizzle@gmail.com. We read every message. In the game, Settings → Send feedback writes the email for you, with your game version. The same address helps with Daisy Grid, Pour Palette, Word Grove and Woolly Logic.

Delete your online profile

Home → Settings (the gear, top left) → Delete online profile → Delete it. The button appears only while online play is on. More: Delete your Brick Rivals profile.

Report an ad

Settings → Report an ad (on iPhone), or email us which ad you saw and when.

Ads and privacy choices

Where the law requires it, Settings → Privacy Choices changes your ad consent. On iPhone, your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls tracking. More: Privacy Policy.

Sound

On iPhone the game follows the Ring/Silent switch (Silent mode, on iPhones with an Action button): turn silent off to hear the game. Music you play in another app keeps playing alongside it. Sound and Music in the game's Settings turn its sound effects and music on and off.

Progress

Your coins and unlocks are saved on your phone. Deleting the game deletes them.
