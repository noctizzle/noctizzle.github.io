Delete your Brick Rivals online profile

Brick Rivals by Noctizzle

Brick Rivals only goes online if you choose to play ranked duels or see the global leaderboards. It then uses an anonymous online profile, which is the only account Brick Rivals creates. It has no email address or password. Your profile and the data linked to it are deleted in either of these ways, and you never need to contact us or reinstall the game.

If you have the game: delete it now

  1. Open Brick Rivals and go to the Home screen.
  2. Tap the gear icon (top left) to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete online profile.
  4. Tap Delete it to confirm. The game shows "Online profile deleted."

The profile is deleted straight away. Brick Rivals then stays offline: it won't create a new profile or send scores unless you choose to play online again. If the game says "Could not reach the server", try again when you're connected to the internet.

The Delete online profile button appears only while online play is on. If you don't see it, Brick Rivals has no online profile on this phone.

If you've uninstalled the game: it's deleted automatically

Every profile is deleted automatically, together with everything linked to it, within 30 days of the last time Brick Rivals connected to it. If you've uninstalled the game or cleared its storage, it can't connect any more, so your profile will be gone within 30 days. You don't need to do anything.

While online play is on, opening the game connects to your profile and keeps it. To remove it sooner, delete it in Settings as shown above. If Brick Rivals finds that its profile was deleted, it goes offline and asks you again before making a new one.

What is deleted

  • your anonymous sign-in and its session record;
  • your profile: player name, ball trail, trophies (your duel rating), wins, losses, draws, win streak and best arcade score;
  • all your arcade scores, so you disappear from the leaderboards;
  • every ranked duel you played, which also removes those duels from your opponents' history;
  • any matchmaking entry;
  • any fair-play records on your profile, such as a flag or leaderboard hiding.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your opponents' totals. Their trophies, wins and losses aren't recalculated, but nothing that identifies you is kept with them.
  • Technical logs and backups. Our database provider, Supabase, keeps short-lived technical logs and database backups, which are deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Your phone. Coins, unlocks, your player name and the statistics saved on your phone stay there until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.

Good to know

  • If you bought Remove Ads, deleting your profile doesn't affect it. The purchase belongs to your Google Play account, and you can restore it with Restore Purchases in Settings. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
  • Advertising data is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings app and manage ads at My Ad Center.
  • Daisy Grid, Pour Palette and Word Grove don't have accounts or online profiles. For everything about our games and your data, see our Privacy Policy.
