SkyShot: Valkyrie support

SkyShot: Valkyrie by Noctizzle · for iPhone, iPad and Android

Contact us

Email noctizzle@gmail.com. We read every message. In the game, Settings → About → Email us writes the email for you, with your game version.

How do I play?

When "Your turn" shows, drive left or right with the arrow buttons (driving uses fuel), tilt your barrel with the angle buttons, pick a shot, then hold FIRE. The power bar fills and falls back, so let go when it's where you want it. Knock your rivals' health to zero, or push them off the island, and the last team standing wins. A one-page How to play is always one tap away, even in the middle of a battle.

Why did my shot miss?

Wind bends every shot. The wind dial at the top of the screen shows its direction and strength, and it changes from turn to turn. After each shot a call-out tells you how close you landed, and your last power stays marked on the bar, so you can adjust from there.

What is the Super?

Dealing and taking damage charges your rig's Super. When it's full, pick it like any other shot: the Kettle's Boiler Burst, the Beetle Walker's Thornfall or the Kite Gunner's Gale. Heavier shots push your next turn further back, so choose when to use them.

What are sky events?

Sometimes the weather joins the fight: a gale, falling icicles or embers, a lightning strike or a supply crate you can claim. A warning always shows the turn before anything lands. In Quick Battle you choose how often they happen with Sky events: Off, Sometimes or Often at the bottom of the screen. A few Sky Trials stages have their own weather.

The Locker, Sky Shells and unlocks

Make your pilot and dress your rig in the Locker: paints, decals, shell trails, victory poses, taunts, nameplates and titles. Every skin tone is free. You unlock more by levelling up on the Rewards track, finishing Sky Trials chapters and completing feats, or by spending the Sky Shells you earn by playing in the Locker's daily shop. Looks never change how a rig plays. Nothing in SkyShot is for sale, and Sky Shells can't be bought.

Comfort settings

In Settings: Left-handed controls moves FIRE to the left, Larger text and Reduce flashes make the game easier on the eyes, Screen shake sets how much the camera shakes, Battery saver runs the game at 30 frames a second for a cooler phone and longer battery, and Vibration turns the light taps on phones on or off.

Sound and music

Music and Sound effects in Settings set their volume. On iPhone, the game stays quiet while the Ring/Silent switch is on silent (Silent mode, on iPhones with an Action button).

iPad, tablets and iPhone Duo

SkyShot plays in landscape on phones and tablets and fills the screen on iPad, Android tablets and iPhone Duo. If something looks cut off or hard to tap on your device, email us its model and a screenshot.

Your progress

There is no SkyShot account and nothing to sign in to. Your pilot, Sky Trials stars, level, Sky Shells and unlocks are saved on your device, and deleting the game deletes them. Settings → Reset progress starts again from scratch. We can't restore progress for you, because we never receive it. More: Privacy Policy.
