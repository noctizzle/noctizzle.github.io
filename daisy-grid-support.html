Daisy Grid support

Daisy Grid by Noctizzle · for iPhone, iPad and Android

Contact us

Email noctizzle@gmail.com. We read every message. Please tell us the game's version and whether you play on an iPhone, an iPad or Android. In the game, Settings → Send feedback writes the email for you, with your game version. Settings is the gear on the home screen and in Classic.

How to play

Drag the pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it; clear several at once, or one move after another, for a combo. When none of your pieces fits, the game is over.

  • Classic is the endless game: play for your best score, and pick up where you left off.
  • Garden Journey has 60 levels in six garden beds. Each level has a goal, such as picking the daisies or clearing lines, in a set number of moves. Win a level for up to three petals, and finish a bed to make it bloom.
  • The Daily Challenge is a new level every day. Play it on days in a row to build your streak; one missed day a week is forgiven, so the streak carries on.

Coins and looks

You earn coins by playing: clearing lines, winning levels and finishing the Daily Challenge. Coins are never sold. Spend them on New pieces (a fresh set in the tray), on a Revive when a Classic game is over, on more moves or space in Garden Journey, and on new looks: themes and block styles in Looks. Some looks also unlock as you finish garden beds. Optional short videos give you a free Revive, triple a level's coins or add coins from the coin shop. You never have to watch one.

Report an ad

On iPhone and iPad, use Settings → Report an ad. On Android, email us which ad you saw and when.

Ads and privacy choices

Daisy Grid is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears once you have cleared the first board of the short tutorial, before any ads load, and Settings → Privacy Choices changes your ad consent later. On iPhone and iPad, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking in your device's Settings controls tracking; the game plays the same whichever you choose. More: Privacy Policy.

Sound and music

Sound and Music in the game's Settings turn its sound effects and music on and off. On iPhone the game follows the Ring/Silent switch (Silent mode, on iPhones with an Action button): turn silent off to hear the game. iPads have no Ring/Silent switch, so use Control Centre or the volume buttons. Music you play in another app keeps playing alongside it.

Your progress

There is no account and nothing to sign in to. Your Classic game, Garden Journey, Daily Challenge results, stats, looks and coins are saved on your device, and deleting the game deletes them. We can't restore them for you, because we never receive them.

Devices

Daisy Grid plays on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. It has no purchases on any of them: everything in the game is earned by playing.
