Word Grove support

Word Grove by Noctizzle · for iPhone and Android

Contact us

Email noctizzle@gmail.com. We read every message. In the game, Settings → Send feedback writes the email for you, with your game version. Settings is the gear on the puzzle screen.

What are secrets?

Every puzzle hides a secret: a short saying spelt out by some of its spare letters, the ones no word uses. Find the last word and the secret lights up in gold.

What are bonus words?

Some puzzles also hide an extra word from the theme that isn't on the list. Drag across it like any other word for 5 coins. You never need a bonus word to finish a puzzle, and hints never point to one.

How do hints work?

Tap the Hint button, or any word in the list, to circle that word's first letter. A hint costs 25 coins, and you can take as many as you have coins for. Each puzzle also has one free hint, which you earn by watching an optional video when you are short of coins. Free hint videos unlock after you finish your first puzzle. Once you have used a puzzle's free hint, more hints cost 25 coins. The Hint button shows its price. You earn coins by finishing puzzles and finding bonus words.

What can I spend coins on?

Hints and, from version 1.1, grove decorations. A hint costs 25 coins and circles a word's first letter. There are 14 decorations, from a Wooden Bench to a Duck Pond, at 150 to 600 coins each, and you buy each one once. Tap Grove, then Decorate your grove, and the game asks you to confirm before it spends anything. The five dearest unlock as your grove grows: Leafy Oak, Grand Oak and Ancient Grove. You earn coins by finishing puzzles, finding bonus words, finishing Seed Packets and collecting the Daily Reward. You can also tap your coins to open the Coin Shop and watch an optional video for 50 coins: from version 1.1, up to three a day, and then the button asks you to come back tomorrow. Coins are only ever earned in the game, never bought.

What are Seed Packets?

From level 6, the Packs button opens your Seed Packets, one for each theme you have played. Each packet has three puzzles, Small, Medium and Large, with a star for each. Finish all three for 50 coins.

How do I change the puzzle style?

Settings → Puzzle style. Relaxed keeps the boards smaller, with words that only read forwards. Classic is the full grove: boards grow and words run every way. Expert brings bigger boards sooner, with more words to find. A puzzle you have already started keeps its style until you finish it; every other puzzle uses the new one. The daily puzzle stays the same for everyone.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

Daily reminders are optional and stay off until you say yes. The game offers them after your second daily puzzle, or you can turn them on any time in Settings → Daily reminder and choose the morning (9 am), afternoon (2 pm) or evening (7 pm). Send a test reminder checks that they arrive. You get at most one a day, and none once today's daily is done.

To stop them, switch off Settings → Daily reminder in the game. You can also turn off notifications for Word Grove in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Word Grove (on Android, Settings → Apps → Word Grove → Notifications). If you turn notifications off there, the game's switch turns itself off too. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, and nothing about them is sent to us.

Game Center

On iPhone, Word Grove has three Game Center leaderboards, Fastest Daily, Daily Streak and Puzzles Solved, and achievements to earn as your grove grows. It's optional: open Settings → Game Center to see them, or tap Sign in to Game Center if you're signed out. Your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center, not to us. Game Center is Apple's, so it isn't in the Android game.

Why does the game look different at Halloween and in winter?

Word Grove dresses up for the seasons, by your phone's date: the Spooky Grove at Halloween, until 2 November, and the Frosty Grove from 1 December to 6 January, with snow, winter daily puzzles and a snowman to build. Prefer the everyday look? Switch off Settings → Winter look (or Halloween look) while the season is on, and each puzzle shows its own theme's painted scene again (see the next answer). The seasonal daily puzzles and your snowman carry on either way.

Why does the scenery change from puzzle to puzzle?

From version 1.1, each theme is set in a painted scene: a sunny meadow, the seaside, a cosy kitchen, a starry twilight, a farm or a woodland clearing. The scene changes when a new puzzle starts, never in the middle of one. In a season, the Halloween or winter look takes over from the scenes.

Report an ad

Settings → Report an ad (on iPhone), or email us which ad you saw and when.

Ads and privacy choices

Word Grove is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message appears once you have finished your first puzzle, and Settings → Privacy Choices changes your ad consent later. On iPhone, your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls tracking; the game plays the same whichever you choose. More: Privacy Policy.

Sound and music

On iPhone the game follows the Ring/Silent switch (Silent mode, on iPhones with an Action button): turn silent off to hear the game. Music you play in another app keeps playing alongside it. Sound and Music in the game's Settings turn its sound effects and music on and off.

Your progress

There is no Word Grove account and nothing you need to sign in to. Your level, daily puzzles, grove and coins are saved on your phone, and deleting the game deletes them. We can't restore them for you, because we never receive them.
